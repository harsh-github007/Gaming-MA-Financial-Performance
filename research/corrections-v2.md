# Corrections in v2

I revised this paper as a descriptive case study of four acquisition programmes. The comparisons show changes around the deals; they do not estimate what would have happened without them.

- I corrected Playdemic's closing date to 20 September 2021 and distinguished the agreement date from completion.
- I added an EA FY2018–20 baseline wholly before the programme, alongside the legacy FY2019–21 comparison.
- I defined the revenue measure as an annualised ratio of three-year averages, rather than endpoint CAGR or organic growth.
- I separated Nazara's EBITDA-style margin from US operating margins and qualified the interpretation of goodwill impairment and cash-flow ratios.
- I removed causal claims about acquisition success, payment method and market foresight.

The [revised manuscript](revised-paper.tex) contains the full reasoning and references. The [audit script](../analysis/reproduce-audit.py) checks 108 stored pre/post fields against the supplied financial CSV; it checks calculation consistency, not certification of every input against its filing. The [verified calculations](../analysis/verified-calculations.json) include timing sensitivities.

The original PDF is retained as a historical version. The revised manuscript is the current text.
