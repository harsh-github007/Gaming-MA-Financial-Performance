With four deals, no test can show a statistically significant average effect (the smallest possible two-sided p-value of a sign or Wilcoxon test on four firms is 0.125), so the results are case evidence, not estimates of an average effect.

Notes: Electronic Arts' FY2020 net income includes a one-off tax benefit, which inflates its net margin, return on assets and return on equity before the deals; operating margin is not affected. Nazara's operating profit is operating profit before depreciation, interest and tax as reported by Screener.in, and its current assets and liabilities are not available.
