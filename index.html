What the deals left on the balance sheet

Goodwill is the part of a purchase price above the value of the assets bought. If the acquisition disappoints, it is written down. Take-Two wrote down $2.3 billion in FY2024 and $3.5 billion in FY2025, mostly tied to Zynga.
